An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back".

After four billion years of wishes, howls and late-night promises, the Moon
finally replies — and it turns out nobody has ever wished anything back for
the Moon itself. The oldest unpaid listener in the sky wants one wish of
its own.

## Entry

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever once wished ME anything back.

## How it works

Run with Node 22.18+:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
